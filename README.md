# EU AI Act Compliance Lab

## Project Overview

This project applies the EU Artificial Intelligence Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, to a simulated AI system called EugAI, a customer service chatbot operated by EugTech Solutions Ltd.

The project demonstrates how an organisation can assess AI system classification, identify applicable obligations, implement Article 50 transparency controls, prepare for High Risk AI obligations, assess General Purpose AI model dependencies, document fundamental rights considerations, define incident reporting procedures, and assess penalty exposure.

## Project Objectives

The objectives of this project are to:

- Classify EugAI under the EU AI Act risk-tier structure
- Document why EugAI is currently Limited Risk
- Identify Article 50 transparency obligations for chatbots
- Create a user-facing AI transparency notice
- Assess what would be required if EugAI became High Risk
- Prepare a High Risk technical documentation template
- Assess GPAI model provider due diligence obligations
- Complete a voluntary Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment
- Define an AI Act incident reporting procedure
- Assess financial penalty exposure
- Map EU AI Act obligations to NIST AI RMF, ISO 27001, and GDPR

## EU AI Act Risk Tier Summary

| Risk Tier | Description | EugAI Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Unacceptable Risk | AI practices banned under Article 5 | EugAI does not perform prohibited practices |
| High Risk | AI systems listed in Annex III or embedded in regulated products | EugAI is not High Risk in current customer service deployment |
| Limited Risk | AI systems with transparency obligations, including chatbots | EugAI is Limited Risk because it interacts with natural persons |
| Minimal Risk | AI systems with no mandatory obligations | Not applicable because EugAI is a chatbot and Article 50 applies |

## Current Compliance Position

EugAI is classified as a Limited Risk AI system because it is a customer service chatbot that interacts with natural persons.

The main current obligation is Article 50 transparency.

EugAI must:

- Inform users they are interacting with AI
- Provide the disclosure clearly at the start of the interaction
- Avoid denying that it is an AI system
- Provide understandable transparency information
- Allow users to escalate to a human where needed

## High Risk Readiness

Although EugAI is currently Limited Risk, the project includes a High Risk gap assessment.

This is included because EugAI could become High Risk if used for:

- Employment screening
- Education access or assessment
- Credit or insurance decisions
- Access to essential services
- Biometric identification or categorisation
- Law enforcement support
- Migration or asylum decisions
- Decisions affecting legal rights or fundamental rights

The High Risk gap assessment shows readiness requirements for risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity, conformity assessment, and EU database registration.
