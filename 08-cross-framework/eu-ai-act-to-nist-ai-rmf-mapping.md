# EU AI Act to NIST AI RMF Mapping

## Purpose

This document maps EU AI Act obligations to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework.

The purpose is to show how EU AI Act compliance obligations can be supported by AI risk management activities across the NIST AI RMF functions: GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, and MANAGE.

## Framework Relationship

The EU AI Act is a binding legal regulation.

NIST AI RMF is a voluntary AI risk management framework.

The EU AI Act defines legal obligations, while NIST AI RMF provides a practical methodology for identifying, measuring, managing, and governing AI risks.

## Mapping Table

| EU AI Act Area | NIST AI RMF Function | Connection |
|---|---|---|
| Article 6 - Risk Classification | MAP | NIST MAP supports understanding the AI system context, use case, affected users, and risk category |
| Article 9 - Risk Management | MAP and MEASURE | NIST MAP identifies AI risks and MEASURE evaluates their likelihood, impact, and control effectiveness |
| Article 10 - Data Governance | GOVERN and MAP | NIST GOVERN supports documentation and accountability, while MAP identifies data-related risks |
| Article 11 - Technical Documentation | GOVERN | NIST GOVERN supports documented AI system information, model documentation, policies, and evidence |
| Article 12 - Record Keeping | MEASURE | NIST MEASURE supports monitoring, logging, evaluation, and evidence collection |
| Article 13 - Transparency | GOVERN | NIST GOVERN supports transparency, model documentation, user information, and accountability |
| Article 14 - Human Oversight | MANAGE | NIST MANAGE supports oversight, escalation, incident response, and ongoing risk treatment |
| Article 15 - Accuracy, Robustness, and Cybersecurity | MEASURE | NIST MEASURE supports testing, red teaming, bias evaluation, hallucination tracking, and security assessment |
| Article 27 - Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment | MAP and MEASURE | NIST MAP identifies affected groups and MEASURE evaluates possible impacts |
| Article 50 - Transparency for Chatbots | GOVERN | NIST GOVERN supports AI disclosure, accountability, transparency, and user awareness |
| Article 73 - Serious Incident Reporting | MANAGE | NIST MANAGE supports incident response, corrective action, and continual improvement |

## Key Observations

- EU AI Act compliance requires legal classification and obligation mapping.
- NIST AI RMF helps structure the risk management activities that support compliance evidence.
- NIST MAP is useful for classification, context, affected populations, and risk identification.
- NIST MEASURE is useful for bias testing, robustness, accuracy, and evaluation.
- NIST MANAGE is useful for incident response, remediation, and ongoing monitoring.
- NIST GOVERN is useful for policy, accountability, transparency, and documentation.

## Summary

The EU AI Act defines what legal obligations apply to AI systems.

NIST AI RMF provides a practical governance and risk management method to help implement and evidence those obligations.

For EugAI, the strongest NIST AI RMF links are Article 50 transparency, Article 9 risk management readiness, Article 15 robustness and cybersecurity, and Article 73 incident response.
