## Current Risk Position

EugAI is currently classified as Limited Risk.

The most relevant penalty category for its current deployment is the Article 50 transparency obligation category.

Current Article 50 controls reduce exposure because:

- Users are informed that they are interacting with AI
- Disclosure appears at the start of the interaction
- EugAI must not deny that it is an AI system
- The transparency notice is written in clear language
- Human escalation is available

## High Risk Deployment Warning

If EugAI is expanded into a High Risk use case, penalty exposure increases significantly.

Examples include:

- Employment decisions
- Credit or insurance eligibility
- Education access
- Access to essential services
- Biometric categorisation
- Law enforcement support
- Migration or asylum decisions
- Judicial decision support

Before any High Risk deployment, EugTech must complete:

- Full High Risk gap remediation
- Risk management system evidence
- Data governance documentation
- Annex IV technical documentation
- Human oversight documentation
- Accuracy, robustness, and cybersecurity testing
- Conformity assessment
- EU database registration where applicable

## Remediation Priorities

| Priority | Action | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Prevent prohibited AI use | Avoid highest penalty category |
| High | Do not deploy High Risk use cases without assessment | Avoid High Risk non-compliance |
| High | Maintain Article 50 transparency controls | Avoid current Limited Risk penalty exposure |
| Medium | Maintain compliance evidence | Reduce risk of incorrect information to authorities |
| Medium | Review AI classification after system changes | Prevent missed obligations |
| Medium | Maintain incident reporting readiness | Reduce exposure from delayed response |

## Conclusion

EugTech's current penalty exposure is mainly linked to Article 50 transparency obligations because EugAI is currently Limited Risk.

The High Risk gap assessment is important because any future expansion of EugAI into employment, credit, education, essential services, biometric, law enforcement, migration, or justice use cases would significantly increase compliance obligations and potential penalty exposure.
